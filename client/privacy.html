Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Overview

MakeWorking.com ("we", "us", "our") provides remote desktop hosting services. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you interact with our customer support channels, including WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram Direct Messages, powered by our CSS (Customer Support Service) Agent.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us through any of our messaging channels, we may collect:

  • Contact information: phone number (WhatsApp), Facebook/Instagram profile name and ID.
  • Message content: text messages, images (e.g. payment screenshots), and file attachments you send through supported channels.
  • Transaction data: payment receipts, order details, and support ticket history.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, and interaction logs with our customer support dashboard.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your customer support inquiries and provide requested services.
  • Process orders, verify payments, and deliver remote desktop credentials.
  • Send service-related notifications (e.g. delivery, renewal reminders).
  • Improve our support quality and train our AI-assisted response system.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Meta Platforms: message content is processed through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API, Messenger Platform, and Instagram Graph API in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Service providers: trusted third-party hosting and infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Specifically:

  • Message histories are retained for 90 days from the last interaction to maintain support continuity.
  • Order and payment records are retained for 12 months for accounting and dispute resolution.
  • Webhook and system logs are retained for 30 days for debugging and security monitoring.

Data is purged automatically after these retention periods unless a longer retention is required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Webhook signature verification (X-Hub-Signature-256) to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Basic authentication on all admin and dashboard endpoints.
  • Regular security audits and access control reviews.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Object to processing of your data for specific purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
